## 🔒 Security & Privacy Features

### 1. 🔐 End-to-End Encryption
Your most sensitive data is protected:
- **Journal entries** encrypted with AES-256-GCM
- **Mood notes** encrypted before storage
- **Key derivation** using PBKDF2 with 100,000 iterations
- **No server access**: Encryption happens on your device
- **Only you can read**: Even we can't decrypt your data

### 2. 🛡️ Password Security
- **Strong hashing**: SHA-256 with salt
- **Never transmitted**: Stays on your device
- **No plaintext storage**: Always encrypted
- **Secure comparison**: Protected against timing attacks

### 3. 🔒 Session Management
- **Secure storage**: sessionStorage with security headers
- **Auto-logout**: Protection on device loss
- **One device at a time**: Extra security
- **Clear indication**: Know when you're logged in

### 4. 🚫 XSS & CSRF Protection
- **Input validation**: All user input validated
- **Output escaping**: React's built-in protection
- **CSRF tokens**: Form protection
- **Content policies**: Strict security headers
