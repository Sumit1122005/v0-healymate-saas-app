# HealyMate - Getting Started Guide

Welcome to HealyMate, your AI-powered mental health and wellness companion! This guide will help you understand and get the most out of the platform.

## Quick Start

### First Time Users

1. **Create an Account**
   - Visit the login page
   - Click "Don't have an account? Sign up"
   - Enter your email and create a password
   - Your account is created instantly (no verification needed for demo)

2. **Explore the Dashboard**
   - You'll see an overview of your wellness stats
   - All your data is encrypted and stored locally on your device
   - No information is sent to external servers

3. **Start Your Journey**
   - Click "Journal" to write your first entry
   - Click "Mood Tracker" to log how you're feeling
   - Try "Meditation" for a guided session
   - Visit "Goals" to set wellness objectives

## Features Overview

### 📔 Journal - Your Personal Wellness Space

**What it does:**
- Write private journal entries with AI sentiment analysis
- All entries are encrypted with your password
- Search through past entries
- Track your emotional patterns

**How to use:**
1. Click "Journal" in the sidebar
2. Click "New Entry" or the floating action button
3. Add a title and write your thoughts
4. Select your current mood (1-10 scale)
5. Click "Save" - your entry is now encrypted
6. AI will analyze the sentiment and display insights

**Privacy:**
- Only you can read your entries
- Encryption uses AES-GCM (bank-level security)
- Your password is the only key to decrypt

### 📊 Mood Tracker - Understanding Your Patterns

**What it does:**
- Log your daily mood with context
- Visualize mood trends over time
- Identify triggers and patterns
- Track correlations with journal entries

**How to use:**
1. Click "Mood Tracker" in the sidebar
2. Click "Log Mood" or the floating action button
3. Select your mood (1-10 scale)
4. Add activities or notes explaining why
5. Submit - your mood is recorded

**Insights:**
- View 7-day, 30-day, or all-time trends
- See average mood and mood distribution
- Identify your most common mood
- Find patterns in your emotional cycle

### 🧘 Meditation - Guided Wellness Sessions

**What it does:**
- Access a library of guided meditations
- Multiple durations (5-30 minutes)
- Different types: breathing, body scan, mindfulness, sleep
- Track your meditation practice

**How to use:**
1. Click "Meditation" in the sidebar
2. Browse available sessions
3. Select duration and type
4. Click "Start Session"
5. Follow the guided instructions
6. Session is marked complete when finished

**Benefits:**
- Reduce anxiety and stress

- Build consistent practice habit

## Data & Privacy

### How Your Data is Protected

**Encryption:**
- All sensitive data is encrypted with AES-GCM
- Uses your password as the encryption key
- Industry-standard PBKDF2 key derivation
- Even we can't read your data without your password

**Storage:**
- Data stored locally on your device
- No cloud backup by default
- Browser's IndexedDB database
- Survives device restarts
- Only deleted when you logout

**Sessions:**
- Session stored in browser
- Auto-clears when you logout
- No tracking across sessions
- No cookies sent to external sites

### What We DON'T Do

- ❌ Don't sell your data
- ❌ Don't track your activities
- ❌ Don't share with third parties
- ❌ Don't require real identity
- ❌ Don't use external analytics
- ❌ Don't store passwords in plain text

### Troubleshooting

**Can't login?**
- Check your email and password
- Password is case-sensitive
- Clear browser cache if stuck
- Try a different browser

**Missing entries?**
- Data is device-specific
- Different browsers don't share data
- Clearing browser data deletes everything
- Log in on same device/browser

**Slow performance?**
- Clear old entries if you have many
- Close other browser tabs
- Restart your browser
- Update to latest browser version

**Meditation not starting?**
- Check device volume
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Try in desktop browser for better support
- Refresh page if stuck

## FAQ

**Q: Is my data really private?**
A: Yes! Encryption happens on your device. Even our servers (if we had them) couldn't read your data.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes, you can download your data anytime. Feature coming soon in Settings.

**Q: What if I forget my password?**
A: Unfortunately, without your password, your encrypted data can't be recovered. Use a password manager to store it securely.

**Q: Can I use HealyMate offline?**
A: Yes! Once loaded, it works completely offline. Changes sync when you're back online.

**Q: Can therapists access my journal?**
A: No. Therapist integration will be your choice to manually share. They never access your encrypted data.

**Q: Is HealyMate a replacement for therapy?**
A: No. It's a complement to professional mental health care, not a substitute. Use alongside therapy, not instead of it.

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Log out or clear your browser data. We don't have a database to delete from (data is only on your device).

**Q: Can I transfer my data to another device?**
A: Future feature: export and import functionality. Currently, create new entries on each device.

**Q: Is HealyMate available on mobile?**
A: Yes! Works great on mobile browsers. Native apps coming soon.
